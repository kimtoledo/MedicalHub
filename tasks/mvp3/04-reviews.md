# Reviews

> **Status:** 🔜 Future — MVP 3

---

## What & Why

Patient reviews build trust for clinics and dentists. Reviews must be tied to completed appointments to prevent spam, and clinics must be able to respond publicly.

---

## Done looks like

- Only patients with a completed appointment at a clinic can leave a review for that clinic or the attending dentist.
- A review contains: star rating (1–5), free-text comment, and date.
- Reviews are moderated before going live (or within a short window with post-publish moderation).
- Clinics can submit one written response to each review.
- Patients can report a review as inappropriate; ToothHub moderators can remove it with a reason.
- Anti-abuse controls: rate limiting, one review per patient per appointment.
- Average rating is displayed on the clinic microsite and dentist profile.

---

## Out of scope

- Incentivized reviews.
- Review syndication to Google / Facebook.
